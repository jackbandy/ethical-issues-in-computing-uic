Read a Book

Everyone in this class picks a book about technology and society, reads it, and shares what they learned. The assignment comes in five parts:

  1. Select a Book to Read! — pick your book and say why
  2. Book Report — a two-page report, with rubric
  3. Book Presentation — a ~10-minute talk, with guidelines, FAQ, and rubric
  4. Public Communication (Your "Unessay") — the creative component
  5. Read the Book — self-report and recommendation

Curious what other students picked? The book gallery collects their selections, along with reviews.
