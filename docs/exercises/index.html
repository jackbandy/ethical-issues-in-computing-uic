Out-of-Class Exercises

Exercises you complete on your own time, between class meetings:

  1. The "Food" in your "Feed" — a guided media diary of an algorithmic feed
  2. Online Account Biopsy — request and read the trace data a platform holds about you
  3. Online Account Scrap Doll — build an actual doll out of what those data capture
  4. Speculative Fiction — write an episode of Black Mirror
  5. Fairness Definition — pick and defend a definition of algorithmic fairness
  6. Personal Commitment — write your own ethics statement for professional practice
