Out-of-Class Exercises

Exercises you complete on your own time, between class meetings:

  1. The "Food" in your "Feed"A guided media diary of an algorithmic feed
  2. Online Account BiopsyRequest and read the trace data a platform holds about you
  3. Online Account Scrap DollBuild an actual doll out of what those data capture
  4. Speculative FictionWrite an episode of Black Mirror
  5. Fairness DefinitionPick and defend a definition of algorithmic fairness
  6. Personal CommitmentWrite your own ethics statement for professional practice
