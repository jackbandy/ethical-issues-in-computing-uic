{%- comment -%} The in-page half of the crawl policy; /robots.txt is the other half. Both say the same thing, because they are read at different moments: robots.txt is asked for before a crawl starts, this tag is found on a page reached some other way — a link someone shared, a URL guessed, a fetch that skipped robots.txt entirely. The site is closed to automated access until 2027 (see docs/robots.txt for the reasoning and the reopening checklist). Include this in the of every layout and every standalone page. noindex — do not put this page in a search index nofollow — do not treat its links as an invitation to crawl further noarchive — do not serve a cached copy nosnippet — do not quote it in results noimageindex — do not index the images on it `noai` and `noimageai` are conventions rather than standards (DeviantArt's, and since picked up by a few crawlers). They cost one attribute and state intent where the standard vocabulary has no word for it. The rel=author link is the humanstxt.org convention: the same head that tells machines to keep out points people at /humans.txt. Deliberately NOT set: `notranslate`. Students read this site, some of them not in their first language, and blocking in-browser translation to discourage machines would take that away from people.
