A choose-your-own-adventure game based on Hermann Hesse's novel *Siddhartha*. Play as Siddhartha, decide which path to take, and see how four ethical lenses respond to your choices.

**[Play the game](https://ajunabia228.github.io/siddhartha-the-road-less-traveled/)**

## About the project

This game is my "unessay" (public communication assignment) for CS 377, Ethics in Technology. It is designed for readers who have never opened the book. The story only mentions characters and events that the player has actually met on their own path.

The question behind it: **What would you do in order to achieve your biggest goal?**

## How to play

1. Open the game and click **Begin the journey**.
2. Read each scene and choose what Siddhartha does next.
3. Read the ethics note under each scene to see how that moment connects to a way of thinking about right and wrong.
4. Reach an ending, check your ethical profile, and try a different path.

## Paths and endings

You can start as a Brahmin's son and become a Samana, follow the Buddha, chase wealth in the city, or walk straight to the river and become a ferryman. There are seven endings:

- The Dutiful Son
- The Hollow Ascetic
- The Follower
- Lost in Sansara
- Chains of Love
- The Teacher of Rules
- Om, the River's Unity

## The four ethical lenses

| Lens | Theory | Core question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duty | Deontology | What principles should I act from, no matter the outcome? |
| Consequences | Utilitarianism | Which choice does the most good for everyone affected? |
| Care | Care ethics | How do I attend to the people who depend on me? |
| Virtue | Virtue ethics | Who am I becoming through my choices? |

Each ending also asks what AI should strive for through the same four lenses.

## Run it locally

No installation is needed. Download or clone this repository and open `index.html` in any web browser.
