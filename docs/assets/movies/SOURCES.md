# Sources & Licenses — `movies/`

Candidate art for the Odysseus / whirlpool (Charybdis) slide that accompanies `odyssey-rights.png`. All four are licensable for course use; two are public domain, two are CC BY-SA (attribution + share-alike required on the photograph).

- **whirlpool-1-fuseli-1794.jpg** — Henry Fuseli, *Odysseus in front of Scylla and Charybdis*, 1794–96, oil on canvas — [Wikimedia Commons: Johann Heinrich Füssli 054.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Johann_Heinrich_F%C3%BCssli_054.jpg) — License: **Public domain** (artist d. 1825); no attribution required — 2024 × 2543 — Odysseus braced in the ship with a spear raised, Scylla's heads above, Charybdis' spray filling the gorge. Painterly, dark, the most dramatic of the four.

- **whirlpool-2-bromley-1806.jpg** — William Bromley after Henry Fuseli, *Odysseus Between Scylla and Charybdis*, 1806, engraving (proof before letters), British Museum 1853,1210.557 — [Wikimedia Commons: William Bromley - Odysseus Between Scylla and Charybdis, 1806.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:William_Bromley_-_Odysseus_Between_Scylla_and_Charybdis,_1806.jpg) — License: **Public domain**; no attribution required — 546 × 726 (**lowest resolution of the four** — fine at half-slide, not for full-bleed) — Odysseus hanging from the fig branch over the whirlpool with the ship's broken timber below; line engraving, so it sits closest to the repo's neutral/grayscale figure convention.

- **whirlpool-3-hiremy-hirschl-1910.jpg** — Adolf Hirémy-Hirschl, *Zwischen Skylla und Charybdis*, c. 1910, pastel — [Wikimedia Commons: Adolf Hiremy-Hirschl - Zwischen Skylla und Charybdis.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adolf_Hiremy-Hirschl_-_Zwischen_Skylla_und_Charybdis.jpg) — License: **CC BY-SA 4.0**, attribution required (credit Adolf Hirémy-Hirschl via Wikimedia Commons / kunkelfineart.de) — 2362 × 3508 — the ship head-on with the crew at the oars in a narrow, churning strait. The only option that puts the *crew* in frame, which is the half of the scene Eurylochus is arguing for.

- **whirlpool-4-allori-1574.jpg** — Alessandro Allori, *Storie di Ulisse: il passaggio dello stretto di Messina tra Scilla e Cariddi*, 1574–76, fresco, Palazzo Portinari-Salviati (Courtyard of the Emperors), Florence; photograph by Francesco Bini (User:Sailko) — [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alessandro_allori,_storie_di_ulisse,_1574-76,_loggetta_est_09_passaggio_dello_stretto_di_messina_tra_scilla_e_cariddi_(l._12)_1,0.jpg) — License: **CC BY-SA 4.0**, attribution required (credit Francesco Bini / Sailko, Wikimedia Commons) — 5094 × 3126 — ship, whirlpool, and Scylla snatching crewmen all legible in one frame; needs cropping out of the ornamental grotesque border.
