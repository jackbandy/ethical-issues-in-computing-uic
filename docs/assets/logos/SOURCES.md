# Sources & Licenses — platform logos

Company logos are **trademarks**, not freely licensed artwork. The SVG files here are
the public-domain vector traces published on Wikimedia Commons (a logo below the
threshold of originality carries no separate copyright, but the trademark still
belongs to its owner). They are used here nominatively — to identify the company
whose moderation figures a slide is reporting — in non-commercial course materials.
Reassess before any other use.

## Generated plates

`*-plate.svg` are generated by [`sandbox/make_platform_plates.py`](../../../sandbox/make_platform_plates.py),
which is the source of truth — edit the script, not the SVGs.

Each plate is a golden rectangle (1000 × 618, per [STYLE.md](../STYLE.md)) filled with that
platform's own primary brand colour, with the wordmark recoloured to a **white monochrome
knockout** and centred at 52% of the plate width. The brand colour carries the
identification, so the logo itself does not need to be in its original colours:

| Plate | Background | Why that colour |
|---|---|---|
| `meta-plate.svg` | `#0064E0` | Meta blue — the gradient anchor inside Meta's own wordmark |
| `tiktok-plate.svg` | `#000000` | TikTok black — the app's own background surface |
| `youtube-plate.svg` | `#FF0000` | YouTube red — the play-button colour |

Recolouring is a monochrome knockout only; no mark is redrawn, restretched, or recombined.
