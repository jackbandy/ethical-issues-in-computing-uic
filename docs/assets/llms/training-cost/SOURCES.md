They are drawn with Matplotlib by `make_figures.py` in this folder, which reads `data.csv` from here too; rebuild with `python3 docs/assets/llms/training-cost/make_figures.py`. ## data.csv

One row per model.

- **Columns:** hardware, GPU-hours, energy (MWh), CO₂e (metric tons), and a low–high cost in USD.
- **Basis columns:** each number has a matching `*_basis` column. Every basis value starts with one of:
  - `reported`: the developer disclosed it.
  - `derived`: simple arithmetic on disclosed values.
  - `published estimate` / `third-party estimate` / `estimate`: from outside researchers.
  - `undisclosed`: nothing public.
- **Links:** `source` names each row's citations in short form, and `source_urls` gives their links in the same order, separated by "; ".
- **Strubell rows:** CO₂e is converted from Table 3's lbs (÷ 2204.62). GPU-hours are Table 3 "Hours" × chip count.
- **Extra rows:** the CSV also covers energy and CO₂e for rows these figures don't plot. Those come from:
  - [Patterson et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.10350), Table 4 (GPT-3: "552 tCO2e … 1287 MWh")
  - [Luccioni et al., *JMLR* 2023](https://jmlr.org/papers/v24/23-0069.html), Table 1 (BLOOM: "Total energy used 433,196 kWh") and Table 3 (CO2e: 24.69 t dynamic, 50.5 t with idle + embodied)
  - [Touvron et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09288), Table 2 (Llama 2 70B: 291.42 tCO₂eq)
  - the [Llama 3.1](https://github.com/meta-llama/llama-models/blob/main/models/llama3_1/MODEL_CARD.md) and [Llama 4](https://github.com/meta-llama/llama-models/blob/main/models/llama4/MODEL_CARD.md) model cards (location-based tons CO₂eq)

## Caveats

- **Cost bases differ.** Strubell, Epoch "cloud", and DeepSeek all price compute at rental rates. Llama 2 70B is left off the chart because Epoch has only an amortized hardware + energy figure for it ($1.10M, "Training compute cost (2023 USD)"), not a cloud one; its row stays in `data.csv` for GPU-hours and CO₂e. Cottier et al. find the cloud-price approach "yields costs that are about twice as large on average."
- **Peer review.** Strubell (ACL), Luccioni (JMLR), and Guo (*Nature*) are peer-reviewed. DeepSeek-V3, Cottier, Patterson, and Llama 2 are arXiv reports. Epoch's values are outside estimates.
