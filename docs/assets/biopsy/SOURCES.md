# Sources & Licenses — `biopsy/`

Demo charts for the week 6 day 2 "Account Biopsy: Demo Analysis" slides. All four are the instructor's own analysis of their TikTok data export (April–October 2025), made for the online account biopsy exercise and first shown in the spring 2026 "09 Here and Now" Keynote deck, where they were embedded as PDFs. Rasterized at 250 dpi, trimmed, and downscaled to at most 1800px.

- **demo-cumulative-watched.png** — "Cumulative TikTok Videos Watched Over Time" — Keynote source `tiktok_cumulative_watched.pdf`
- **demo-heatmap-day-hour.png** — "TikTok Viewing Heatmap: Day of Week × Hour of Day" — Keynote source `tiktok_heatmap_day_hour.pdf`
- **demo-videos-per-month.png** — "TikTok Videos Watched Per Month" — Keynote source `tiktok_videos_per_month.pdf`
- **demo-off-platform-sources.png** — "Top 20 Off-TikTok Data Sources (n=483 total events)" — Keynote source `tiktok_off_platform_sources.pdf`
