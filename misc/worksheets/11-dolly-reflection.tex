\wsquestion{Write a 2--3 sentence \textbf{log line} for your proposed film adaptation of ``Dolly.'' For example, a log line for \textit{Jurassic Park} (1993) was\ldots{} ``An industrialist invites some experts to visit his theme park of cloned dinosaurs.
